Before getting into what not to say, I want to establish a baseline for how to respond to the people who genuinely want to know how you're doing. Don't come off as angry or standoffish just because someone asks, "how are you doing?" They can't possibly know, or probably don't, the series of emotions you're going through.

To those of you reading, this is my best example of the thoughts I've had over this past year. Let it serve as a guide for framing your conversations with someone who's been thrust into the new job market, or forced to search for their purpose.

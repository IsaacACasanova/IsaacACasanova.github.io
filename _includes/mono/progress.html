{%- comment -%} Reading progress for the mono theme: a 2px accent line fixed to the top of the viewport that fills as the reader moves through the post body. Progress is 0 until the body's top reaches the top of the viewport and 1 once its end is on screen, so the bar tracks the words, not the page. A post that fits in the viewport shows no bar. Without JavaScript nothing renders. {%- endcomment -%}
